Expanded Support Conversation System

We'll expand Ocarina of Time's existing support network and establish extensive support options for Majora's Mask, The Wind Waker and Twilight Princess, including relationships between characters from different games.

Supports should explore friendships, family relationships, rivalries, mentorships, ideological disagreements, shared experiences and romantic relationships where appropriate. Villains and monsters should have support options too.

Support Rank Rules

Rank	Relationship progression
C	Initial interaction or establishment of their existing relationship.
B	Growing familiarity, shared experiences or developing conflict.
A	Deep trust, reconciliation, loyalty or mutual understanding.
S	Defining relationship, romance, lifelong friendship, sworn loyalty or powerful familial bond.

Not every relationship needs an S rank. Some should conclude naturally at A or even B.

Alternate transformations generally share their original character's support progression, although certain conversations can require a specific form. Different incarnations of Link, Zelda and Ganondorf have independent relationships.

For clarity, I'll use A and S to indicate the maximum available support rank for each pairing.

9. Support Conversation Writing Examples

The following establish how the actual dialogue should work. Conversations should be character-driven rather than merely explaining their combat bonuses.

Hero of Time × Hero of Winds

C Support

Hero of Winds: Is that the Master Sword?

Hero of Time: Yes.

Hero of Winds: I've got one too. The King told me it belonged to a hero from a long time ago.

Hero of Time: The King?

Hero of Winds: Well, he was a boat when he told me.

Hero of Time: A boat?

Hero of Winds: It's a long story.

The Hero of Time examines the younger hero's sword.

Hero of Time: Where did you find it?

Hero of Winds: Under the sea. In Hyrule Castle.

Hero of Time: Under the sea?

Hero of Winds: You really haven't heard about any of this, have you?

B Support

Hero of Time: What happened to Hyrule?

Hero of Winds: Ganondorf came back. The people waited for the old hero to return, but he never did.

The Hero of Time lowers his sword.

Hero of Winds: The gods flooded the kingdom. That's what the King told me, anyway.

Hero of Time: And the people?

Hero of Winds: Some of them escaped to the mountaintops. That's where our islands came from.

Hero of Time: I see.

Hero of Winds: You couldn't have known.

Hero of Time: No. I suppose not.

A Support

Hero of Winds: You know, everyone kept telling me about you when I started my adventure.

Hero of Time: Did they?

Hero of Winds: I didn't really care. I just wanted my sister back.

Hero of Time: That's a good reason to pick up a sword.

Hero of Winds: Even if it isn't some great destiny?

Hero of Time: Especially then.

Hero of Winds: The King eventually called me the Hero of Winds.

Hero of Time: It suits you.

S Support

Hero of Time: I've been thinking about what you told me.

Hero of Winds: About Hyrule?

Hero of Time: Yes. I spent so much of my life trying to save it. It's difficult to imagine it gone.

Hero of Winds: There are still people living up there. We've got towns, forests, farms. Everything we need.

Hero of Time: Are they happy?

Hero of Winds: Most of them. My grandma is. Aryll too.

The older hero smiles.

Hero of Time: Then perhaps Hyrule survived after all.

Hero of Winds: When this is over, you should come visit Outset.

Hero of Time: I'd like that.

S Rank Bonus — A New Generation: Both heroes gain +3 STR and SKL when within three spaces of one another. Once per battle, either hero can intercept a fatal attack directed at the other.
