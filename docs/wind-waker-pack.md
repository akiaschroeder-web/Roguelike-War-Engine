# The Wind Waker content pack (design doc)

As supplied. Art in `art_sources/wind_waker/`.

The Legend of Zelda: The Wind Waker — Content Pack

The Wind Waker pack introduces naval warfare, aerial units, Rito and Korok armies, wind manipulation and equipment designed around battlefield mobility and enemy disarmament.

As with the previous packs:

* Link, Zelda and Ganondorf receive new incarnations.
* Returning characters and monsters do not receive duplicate units.
* Distinct monster species such as Boko Babas, Bokoblins and Darknuts are included, even when they resemble existing enemies.
* Returning equipment uses existing entries unless its functionality is substantially different.
* Every unit is recruitable, including bosses, deities and monsters.
* Every unit has an assigned battlefield size.

1. Main Characters

Link — Hero of Winds

Race: Hylian Class: Wind Adventurer Size: 1 Role: Versatile Infantry

Personal Skill — Hero of Winds: After successfully dodging a melee attack, Link may perform a parry attack that ignores 50% of the attacker’s DEF.

Proficiencies: Swords, shields, bows, grappling tools, boomerangs and instruments.

Tetra

Race: Hylian Class: Pirate Captain Size: 1 Role: Mobile Commander

Personal Skill — Pirate’s Fortune: Defeated enemies have a 25% increased chance of dropping equipment or valuables. Allied pirates within three spaces gain +2 SKL.

Proficiencies: Cutlasses, daggers, bows and grappling tools.

Princess Zelda — Wind Waker

Race: Hylian Class: Sacred Archer Size: 1 Role: Ranged Magical Support

Personal Skill — Sacred Partnership: When attacking an enemy previously damaged by an ally during the same turn, Zelda’s light attacks ignore 50% of its RES.

Proficiencies: Bows, light magic and staffs.

Tetra and Zelda are alternate forms of the same character, but both are obtainable as independently deployable units.

Ganondorf — King of the Forsaken Fortress

Race: Gerudo Class: Dual-Blade Warlord Size: 1 Role: Offensive Commander

Personal Skill — Desert Wind: After successfully avoiding an attack, Ganondorf immediately counters with both swords. Each strike deals 75% of normal damage.

Proficiencies: Dual swords, greatswords and dark magic.

This incarnation emphasizes swordsmanship and counterattacks rather than the magical power of Ocarina of Time’s Ganondorf.

Wind Waker Boomerang functionality

The existing Boomerang gains a learnable technique called Multi-Target Throw.

* Select up to five targets within four spaces.
* The Boomerang strikes each target sequentially.
* Each strike deals 60% of normal damage.
* Can cut vines, activate switches and retrieve distant items.
* Costs 5 stamina.

This is a new technique, not another Boomerang item.

18. Naval Combat Mechanics

Wind direction

Every naval battlefield has a prevailing wind direction.

Wind relationship	Movement modifier
Moving with the wind	+2 MOV
Moving perpendicular to wind	No modifier
Moving against the wind	−2 MOV
Swift Sail equipped	Always favorable
Submerged vessel	Unaffected

Wind’s Requiem and certain deity abilities can change the wind during combat.

Boarding

Infantry can board allied or hostile vessels using adjacent boarding points, Grappling Hooks or boarding ramps.

Enemy ships can be captured by eliminating their defending crew and occupying the captain’s position.

Captured vessels can be permanently added to the army.

Vessel damage

Ships have individually targetable components.

Component	Effect when destroyed
Hull	Vessel sinks when its HP reaches zero.
Sail	Movement reduced by 50%.
Rudder	Turning and maneuvering become restricted.
Cannon	Corresponding weapon becomes unusable.
Mast	Prevents normal wind-powered movement.

Passengers aboard sinking ships must reach nearby vessels, swim, fly or use rescue abilities. Otherwise, they are defeated.

Weapon scavenging and disarmament

Inspired by Wind Waker’s enemy weapon system:

* Disarmed units drop their equipped weapons onto the battlefield.
* Compatible units can pick up dropped weapons.
* Untrained units can use scavenged weapons with reduced accuracy.
* Heavy weapons still require sufficient CON.
* Dropped weapons can be recovered after winning the battle.

21. Unique Campaign Mechanic — The Great Sea

The dedicated Wind Waker campaign takes place across a procedurally populated 7×7 ocean chart, inspired by the original game’s 49 sea sectors.

● Island● Vessel! Hostile encounter? Unknown encounter

Each sector can contain an island, enemy fleet, merchant vessel, submarine, treasure, recruitment opportunity or random event.

Unlike Termina, the Great Sea does not have a universal countdown. Its primary campaign pressures are supplies, ship condition and navigation.
