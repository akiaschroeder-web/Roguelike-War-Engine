- **Crayk, Bane of Courage** (3, Legendary, Invisible Crustacean) — Invisible Hunter: invisible while its shell is intact; detection reveals it. Abilities: Pincer Crush, Invisible Pursuit, Crayklet Summoning, Shell Defense.
- **Cubus Sisters** (1 each, Epic, Ghost Sorceress; four independently recruitable sisters; may share a base design with colour variations) — Dead Man's Volley: reflect magic projectiles between each other, more damage per reflection. All four deployed: +4 MAG, +3 RES and a combined barrage.
- **Dongorongo, Armored Lizard** (4, Legendary, Armored Beast) — Exposed Interior: frontal armor blocks most damage; when knocked over, explosives hit its mouth. Abilities: Armored Charge, Tail Sweep, Flame Breath, Crushing Stomp.
- **Gleeok, Two-Headed Dragon** — uses the existing Gleeok family with an optional dual fire-and-ice loadout; no new entry.
- **Eox, Ancient Stone Soldier** (5, Mythic, Ancient Construct) — Structural Weakness: several joints can be destroyed independently, reducing its abilities. Abilities: Stone Fist, Earthquake, Crushing Step, Ancient Reconstruction.
- **Bellum, Evil Phantom** (4, Mythic, Life-Draining Demon) — Life Force Consumption: heals HP and MP when a nearby enemy dies. Abilities: Tentacle Strike, Phantom Eye Summoning, Life Force Drain, Spectral Flight, Possession, Malicious Regeneration.
- **Bellumbeck** (1, Mythic, Possessed Duelist) — Possessed Armor: frontal attacks −75%; time-stopping abilities expose Bellum's eye. Abilities: Phantom Sword, Spectral Counter, Possessed Charge, Life Force Slash. A distinct transformation of Linebeck via a possession event; independently recruitable.

## 10. Mechanics
**Phantom Hourglass:** a shared army artifact with a limited time-energy reserve, raised for the run by Sands of Hours; refills between battles; major bosses resist long freezes. Abilities: Time Freeze (5: freeze enemies within 3 for one allied action) · Frozen Battlefield (15: ordinary enemies skip the next enemy phase) · Temporal Escape (8: disengage without retaliation) · Sacred Opening (5: expose Phantom and Bellum weak points).
**Temple of the Ocean King:** recurring, expanding dungeon. Units outside Sacred Safe Zones lose HP each round unless the Hourglass has energy; Phantoms patrol fixed routes; Phantom Eyes reveal hidden units; the Phantom Sword makes Phantoms much easier; cleared floors can offer shortcuts, new treasure and other objectives.
**Spirit upgrades:** Leaf / Neri / Ciela with their gems: 0–9 base, 10 level 1, 20 level 2 (Leaf: fire and physical damage; Neri: damage reduction and barriers; Ciela: sword beams and time abilities).
**Salvage:** treasure charts reveal underwater salvage spots; rewards include ship parts, rare gear, Rupees, Spirit Gems, materials, Sands of Hours.
