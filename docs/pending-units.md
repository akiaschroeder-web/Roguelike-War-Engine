## Potion Shop Lady (Ocarina of Time — Kakariko Village)

Art: `art_sources/era_of_legend/potionShopLady.png` (the old woman in the wheelchair). Proposed ids: template `potionShopLady`, art id `potionShopLady`.

- Race: Hylian · Class: Potion Brewer (Kakariko) · Size: 1 · Rarity: Rare · Role: Healer / Item Support
- Personal skill — **Old Recipes:** potions and medicines she uses heal 30% more, and once per battle she can use a pack item on an ally up to 2 spaces away without using her action.
- Abilities:
  - **Brew:** turns one ingredient (mushroom, monster part, bug, Odd Mushroom) into a random potion at the end of a battle.
  - **Bitter Tonic:** an adjacent ally recovers 8 + MAG HP and is cured of poison and sleep.
  - **Wheelchair Rush:** moves up to 3 spaces in a straight line on flat ground; an enemy in the way takes STR + 4 damage and is pushed back one space.
  - **Mixing Shop:** at camp, swaps any two potions in the convoy for one stronger potion.
- Equipment: staffs and thrown flasks; her own item **Wheelchair Spikes** (accessory: +3 DEF, enemies that hit her in melee take 2 damage).
- Base stats (HP STR MAG SKL SPD DEF RES LCK CON MOV STA MP): 21 4 15 13 5 5 16 14 4 4 12 32
- Growths (proposal): HP 45, STR 15, MAG 55, SKL 45, SPD 20, DEF 20, RES 50, LCK 50
- Recruitment: OoT Kakariko Village events, and any campaign's potion shop event after the first region.
