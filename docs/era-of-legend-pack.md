# The Legend of Zelda — Era of Legend Content Pack (A Link to the Past, Link's Awakening, Oracle of Seasons, Oracle of Ages)

Design doc as supplied by the user (condensed; every value kept). Build once all art has arrived.

## User art instructions (binding)
- **Link has multiple arts and should switch between them depending on his equipped weapon** (sword + shield default; jumping sword pose; Rod of Seasons pose; Harp of Ages pose).
- **Zelda has 2 arts that can be used freely** (ordinary random/choosable variants).

Priorities: important characters, major bosses, iconic equipment, unique mechanics. One new Link for all four games. Returning monsters/bosses/equipment reuse existing entries. Only five new ordinary monsters. Every new character and boss is recruitable.

## 1. Main Characters
**Link — Hero of Legend.** Hylian, Legendary Adventurer, size 1, Legendary, All-Rounder. Personal — Four Adventures: equipping tools from different Era of Legend games gives +1 SKL and LCK per represented game. Abilities: Pegasus Dash (straight-line charge, damages the first enemy hit); Secret Discovery (reveals nearby hidden passages and treasure); Hurricane Spin (powerful spin while moving); Legendary Adaptability (equipped utility tools cost 20% less STA and MP).

**Princess Zelda — Era of Legend.** Hylian, Sacred Maiden, size 1, Legendary, Magical Support. Personal — Bloodline of the Sages: nearby allied sages and sacred magic users +3 MAG/RES. Abilities: Sacred Light, Maiden's Blessing, Seal Darkness, Telepathic Guidance.

**Ganon — King of Darkness.** Demon, Trident Warlord, size 3, Mythic, Heavy Magical Assault. Personal — Power of the Triforce: +5 STR/MAG above 50% HP; below 50% HP, +4 SPD and can extinguish nearby light sources. Abilities: Trident Throw, Blazing Bat Swarm, Dark Teleportation, Earthquake, Darkness Shroud, Demonic Restoration. Silver Arrows are especially effective. The resurrected Ganon of the linked Oracle games is this same unit with a berserk moveset.

## 12. Mechanics
**Light World / Dark World:** some maps have two linked versions with different terrain, enemies and routes at the same coordinates; portals or items move units between them; some obstacles exist in only one world; units without a Moon Pearl can suffer a Dark World transformation (demonic/compatible monsters immune); the Magic Mirror returns to the Light World. Link's rabbit form is a temporary status, not a recruitable Link.
**Seasons:** the Rod of Seasons and Din change the battlefield season (spring flowers launch units across obstacles and boost vegetation healing; summer, autumn, winter per the spaces above). Changes affect both armies.
**Time:** Harp of Ages melodies — Tune of Echoes (activates Time Portals), Tune of Currents (past → present at the current position when valid), Tune of Ages (switch past/present anywhere valid). Past actions affect the present map (plant trees, open mechanisms, destroy unfinished structures, restore bridges). Nayru learns action resets and cooldown cuts.
**Gasha farming:** plant Gasha Seeds in soft soil; trees mature with battles won, enemies defeated, locations found, quests done; harvest a Gasha Nut (items or rings); the Gasha Ring speeds growth.
**Ring appraisal:** Vasu is a merchant encounter (not a unit) who identifies Mystery Rings for Rupees; duplicates sell; Ring Boxes store unequipped rings outside the convoy.
