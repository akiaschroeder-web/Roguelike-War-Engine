## 11. Mechanics
**Railway combat:** maps with interconnected tracks; trains carry troops, give cannon support, deliver supplies and deploy units at stations; infantry fight independently; switches redirect trains to avoid collisions or force enemy trains onto bad routes.
**Spirit Zelda and possession:** Physical Zelda (magic support) · Spirit Zelda (passes through walls, no physical equipment, resists ordinary physical attacks) · Phantom Zelda (possesses a compatible Phantom, takes its stats and abilities). Possession needs the target Phantom exposed by a sacred weapon or stunned by an ally. If the possessed Phantom is destroyed, Zelda returns to spirit form instead of dying; her spirit is vulnerable to sacred and demonic attacks.
**Passenger and freight missions:** transport passengers, deliver fragile cargo, escort merchant trains, repair tracks, defend stations; rewards Rupees, Force Gems, gear, recruits.
**Stamp collection:** Elder Niko's Stamp Book records stations and landmarks: 5 stamps → gear and supplies · 10 → Shield of Antiquity · 15 → rare train component · 20 → legendary equipment.
