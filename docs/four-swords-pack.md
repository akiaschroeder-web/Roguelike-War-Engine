# Four Swords & Four Swords Adventures — Content Pack (user spec, condensed)

A combined expansion: cooperative combat, coordinated attacks between the four Links, Force Gems, the Four Sword Sanctuary, and the forces of Vaati and Ganon.
- There are exactly four recruitable Links and one Zelda, shared across both games.
- The Four Sword and all five Vaati forms from the Minish Cap pack carry over; no duplicate equipment entries.
- Every unit is recruitable (the standing rule).
- The Links' specialisations, the generic military classes and the recruitable Big Bomb are custom designs. The boss roster has not been checked for completeness.
- The Gnat Hat uses the Minish Cap Micro unit system.

## 1. The four Links (size 1, Legendary)
- **Green Link**: Four Sword Leader; balanced infantry and commander.
  - United Courage: the other three Links within 4 get +2 STR/SKL/DEF.
  - Coordinated Strike (6 STA): another nearby Link joins the attack.
  - Hero's Rally (5 STA): nearby allies +2 STR/DEF for 3 turns.
  - Sword Beam (5 STA): ranged, at full HP.
- **Red Link**: Four Sword Warrior; heavy assault.
  - Burning Courage: +4 STR below 50% HP.
  - Power Slash (6 STA): 160% damage.
  - Guard Breaker (8 STA): ignores 50% DEF.
  - Reckless Charge (8 STA): charges 4 spaces and strikes hard, at −3 DEF until his next turn.
- **Blue Link**: Four Sword Scout; mobile flanker.
  - Swift Courage: moves 2 more spaces after a kill.
  - Quick Slash (4 STA): two hits at 75%.
  - Rolling Assault (5 STA): passes through the enemy's space and strikes from behind.
  - Evasive Step (4 STA): +30 Avoid until his next turn.
- **Purple Link**: Four Sword Mystic; magic and support.
  - Mystic Courage: sword beams and magical tools +25% damage.
  - Mystic Slash (5 MP): sword damage from MAG.
  - Charged Beam (10 MP): piercing magical beam.
  - Hero's Blessing (8 MP): heals 20% HP and removes one negative status.

## 2. Other named characters
- **Princess Zelda** (1, Legendary, Sacred Princess). Seal of Light: stronger sacred attacks; nearby dark enemies are weakened.
- **Shadow Link** (1, Legendary, Shadow Swordsman). Shadow Reflection: temporary shadow duplicates; travels through dark terrain.
  - He is a separate entry from Dark Link: his own personality, shadow duplication, giant bombs and Dark World abilities.
- **Ganon, Four Swords Adventures** (3, Mythic, Trident Demon). King of Darkness: banishes enemies into the Dark World and empowers allied monsters.
- **Kaepora Gaebora**: the existing Ocarina of Time unit, no new entry. kaeporaGaeboraFS.webp becomes an alt art.
- **Six Shrine Maidens** (1, Epic). The elemental specialities are custom.
  - Green, Forest Blessing: HP regeneration and poison removal.
  - Red, Flame Blessing: stronger fire attacks and fire resistance.
  - Blue, Water Blessing: restores HP and MP.
  - Purple, Shadow Blessing: better concealment and magic resistance.
  - Yellow, Radiant Blessing: removes curses; stronger sacred attacks.
  - White, Protective Blessing: magical barriers around allies.

## 3. Generic units (custom military classes)
- Hyrule Spearman (1, Common). Spear Formation: +DEF next to other spearmen.
- Hyrule Bombardier (1, Uncommon). Military Explosives: more bomb damage and blast radius.
- Hyrule Captain (1, Rare). Military Command: nearby generic Hyrule soldiers +2 STR/SKL.

## 4. Monsters
- Ball and Chain Trooper (1, Uncommon). Chain Reach: attacks at range 2 and pulls the target closer. A lighter version of the Minish Cap Ball and Chain Soldier.
- Big Bomb (2, Rare). Massive Detonation: a living bomb that explodes after a countdown. A custom recruitable adaptation of Shadow Link's giant bombs.
- Terrorpin (1, Uncommon). Armored Shell: resists frontal attacks until flipped over.
- Geldman (1, Uncommon). Sand Ambush: burrows under sand and emerges beneath enemies.

Returning: Darknuts, Stalfos, Moblins, Wizzrobes, Like Likes, ChuChus, Keese, Octoroks, Tektites, Hinox and the other established enemies.

## 5. Bosses
- **Vaati, Wind Mage form** (3, Mythic, Wind Sorcerer; magical artillery and battlefield control). His floating wind-demon form from Four Swords, separate from the Minish Cap forms.

  - Cyclone (10 MP): a moving tornado.
  - Vacuum (8 MP): pulls surrounding units toward him.
  - Wind Barrier (12 MP): deflects projectiles for 2 turns.
  - Eye Beam (10 MP): piercing beam.
  - Great Tornado (25 MP): scatters units over a large area with heavy wind damage.
- **Delu Zol** (3, Epic, Giant Slime). Dividing Body: splits into slime summons after losing enough HP. Abilities: Crushing Bounce, Slime Division, Gel Absorption, Body Regeneration.
- **Gouen** (4, Legendary, Volcanic Monster). Molten Body: immune to fire and lava; heals while on lava. Abilities: Flame Breath, Lava Burst, Crushing Slam, Molten Armor.
- **Dera Zol** (3, Legendary, Elemental Slime). Shifting Defense: its elemental weakness changes during battle. Abilities: Elemental Shift, Slime Barrage, Crushing Bounce, Gel Recovery.
- **Frostare** (3, Legendary, Ice Guardian). Frozen Defense: protective ice that must be melted or shattered. Abilities: Ice Beam, Frozen Barrier, Blizzard, Frost Regeneration.

Weapons and tools:

- Fire Rod Lv. 2: Epic; an upgrade to the existing Fire Rod that leaves persistent flames.

## 8. Four Link formation system
- Unlocked with at least 2 Links deployed. Formations: Box, Line, Column and Cross.
- Box: +4 DEF/RES to all four Links, for 4 CP.
- Commands:

| Command | Needs | Effect |
|---|---|---|
| Formation March | 2 Links | They move together |
| Combined Spin | 2 Links | Spin attacks together, wider coverage |
| Hero Stack | 2 Links | Stack up to reach high switches |
| Cooperative Throw | 2 Links | Throw a Link across gaps or obstacles |
| Fourfold Strike | 4 Links | All four hit the same target in turn |
| Fourfold Barrier | 4 Links | Protective barrier around them |
| Sacred Seal | 4 Links | Massive sacred damage; briefly disables demonic bosses |

- Formation attacks use up every participant's main action, so they don't give free extra turns.

## 9. Force Power (shared across the army, reset every battle)
- Earned by defeating enemies, completing objectives and finding treasure.
- Thresholds:
  - 500, Force Awakening: the four Links +2 STR/MAG.
  - 1,000, Force Enhancement: Four Sword attacks deal extra sacred damage.
  - 1,500, Force Mastery: stronger cooperative attacks.
  - 2,000, Sacred Awakening: the Four Sword is fully empowered and Sacred Seal is enabled.
- Shadow Link and some enemies can steal or scatter Force Gems when they attack.

## 11. Campaign regions
Four Swords:
1. Sea of Trees: Delu Zol.
2. Talus Cave: Dera Zol.
3. Death Mountain: Gouen.
4. Vaati's Palace: Vaati.

Four Swords Adventures:
1. Whereabouts of the Wind: Lake Hylia, Cave of No Return, Hyrule Castle.
2. Eastern Hyrule: the coast, Village of the Blue Maiden, Eastern Temple.
3. Death Mountain: the foothills, the mountain path, Tower of Flames.
4. Near the Fields: Hyrule Field, the swamp, infiltrating Hyrule Castle.
5. The Dark World: Lost Woods, Kakariko Village, Temple of Darkness.
6. The Desert of Doubt: the desert, Desert Temple, the Pyramid.
7. Frozen Hyrule: Frozen Hyrule, Ice Temple, Tower of Winds.
8. Realm of the Heavens: the realm, Dark Cloud, Palace of Winds.

The final encounters are Vaati, then Ganon.

Camp facilities:
- Four Sword Sanctuary: unlocks the four Links; teaches formation commands.
- Force Shrine: improves Force Power collection and retention.
- Shrine Maiden Sanctuary: magical healing; recruits Maidens.
- Great Fairy Workshop: Lv. 2 upgrades for compatible tools.
