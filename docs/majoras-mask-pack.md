# Majora's Mask content pack (design doc)

As supplied. Art in `art_sources/majoras_mask/`.

The Legend of Zelda: Majora’s Mask — Content Pack

The revised Majora’s Mask pack will be more selective than Ocarina of Time, focusing on what Termina actually introduces to the game: transformation masks, distinctive characters, unusual monsters, time magic and specialized equipment.

The following rules apply throughout the pack:

* No duplicate characters or equipment from Ocarina of Time unless their functionality is substantially different.
* Cremia and Romani are not included as units.
* The Deku are the only race receiving a new generic unit set.
* Every battlefield unit, including bosses and monsters, is recruitable.
* Every unit has an assigned size.
* Newly invented equipment should have a distinctive purpose rather than being another variation of existing swords, armor or consumables.

Termina Link is a separate version of Young Link, with different proficiencies and access to transformation abilities.

Link — Hero of Termina

Personal Skill — Hero of Masks: Changing an ordinary or transformation mask does not consume his primary action once per turn.

Proficiencies: Swords, shields, bows, instruments and adventuring tools.

Deku Link

Personal Skill — Deku Mobility: Can bounce across five consecutive water spaces and use Deku Flowers to glide across obstacles.

Natural abilities:

* Deku Spin — Quick melee attack.
* Bubble Blast — Magical ranged projectile.
* Flower Glide — Temporary aerial movement.
* Deku Nut Drop — Drops a stunning projectile while airborne.

Weak against fire.

Goron Link

Personal Skill — Spiked Roll: Moving three consecutive spaces in a straight line allows him to initiate a rolling attack that damages every enemy along his remaining movement path.

Natural abilities: Goron Punch, Ground Pound, Defensive Curl and Goron Roll.

Immune to environmental heat but unable to enter deep water.

Zora Link

Personal Skill — Aquatic Grace: Gains +3 MOV and +15 Avoidance while occupying water.

Natural abilities: Fin Boomerangs, Zora Kick, Rapid Swimming and Electric Barrier.

Electric Barrier consumes MP and damages adjacent attackers.

Fierce Deity Link

Personal Skill — Wrath of the Deity: Deals 50% additional damage against boss-class units.

Automatically equips the Double Helix Sword and can launch magical sword beams.

Consumes 3 MP per turn while transformed.

Giant Link

Personal Skill — Colossal Strength: Physical attacks damage multiple spaces and destroy ordinary walls.

Natural abilities: Giant Stomp, Sweeping Slash and Giant Throw.

Consumes 4 MP per turn while transformed.

All six forms share experience, levels and earned stat growth when used through transformations. Legendary recruitment encounters can also provide independently deployable versions of the forms.

The Goron and Zora Masks from Ocarina of Time are simple disguises. Their transformation counterparts are completely different items and therefore receive separate entries.

Epic • Head

Resulting Size: 2

Unlocks rolling, ground pounds, punching and defensive curling.

Zora Transformation Mask

Epic • Head

Transforms the wearer into a Zora warrior.

Resulting Size: 1

Unlocks aquatic combat, fin boomerangs and electric barriers.

Mythic • Head

Transforms the wearer into a divine swordsman.

Resulting Size: 2

Mythic • Head

Transforms the wearer into a colossal version of themselves.

Transformations temporarily disable incompatible equipment and preserve the character’s percentage of remaining HP. The transformation fails if there is insufficient space for the new unit footprint.

18. Unique Campaign Mechanic — Three-Day Cycle

The Termina campaign features an optional three-day countdown.

The Moon draws closer with each completed encounter.

Completing encounters advances the clock. Different characters, merchants and recruitment opportunities become available depending on the current day.

The Song of Time resets the cycle at a cost.

Retained	Reset or lost
Recruited units	Defeated regional bosses
Character levels	Completed regional objectives
Equipped gear	Ordinary consumables and ammunition
Boss Remains	Unbanked Rupees
Transformation masks	Unresolved timed quests
Learned skills	Temporary blessings
Permanent upgrades	Current campaign route

Fallen characters remain dead. Resetting time does not reverse permanent death.

Each reset increases enemy difficulty, preventing the player from endlessly exploiting the cycle without consequences.

This mechanic only applies to the dedicated Termina campaign.
