# Echoes of Wisdom — Content Pack (user spec, condensed)

The pack covers summoning Echoes, manipulating battlefield objects, the Still World, and Zelda as an unconventional frontline commander.
- The user said it can be built at the same time as Four Swords.
- One new Zelda and one new Link.
- Existing monsters become Echo-compatible rather than getting separate Echo entries.
- Every unit is recruitable (the standing rule).
- The Seismic Talus split, the recruitable Automatons, the Echo Capacity progression and the stats are custom designs.

Zelda's abilities:
- Create Echo (5 MP): summon a learned creature or object.
- Dismiss Echo (0): remove an active Echo and free its capacity.
- Bind (5 MP): ties Zelda's movement to an enemy or object.
- Reverse Bond (5 MP): Zelda follows a bound creature or moving object.
- Swordfighter Form (5 Energy): she becomes a capable physical fighter for a while.
- Restoration (15 MP): repairs a group of unstable Rift spaces with Tri's help.

Link's abilities:
- Mighty Slash (5 STA): powerful sword attack.
- Mighty Shot (5 STA): high-Hit bow attack.
- Mighty Bomb (6 STA): throws an equipped bomb.
- Heroic Spin (8 STA): hits surrounding enemies.
- Rift Breaker (10 STA): double damage against Rift creatures.

## 7. Echo summoning (game-wide system)
- **Learning Echoes:**
  - Defeating eligible monsters unlocks their Echo in the Echo Compendium; objects are learned by exploring.
  - The Compendium draws on the whole monster database across all packs. Named characters, deities, major bosses and unique story units can't normally be learned.
- **Capacity:**
  - Echoes take no roster or deployment slot, but each uses part of the summoner's Echo Capacity.
  - A defeated or dismissed Echo frees its capacity.
- **Limits:**
  - Echoes gain no permanent EXP, can't trade gear or use pack items, and can't summon further Echoes.
  - Enemy Echoes give no recruitment or loot, and allied Echo losses don't count as casualties.
- **Strength:** monster Echoes have 75% of the original's HP, STR, MAG, DEF and RES; movement and utility are unchanged.
- **Cost table:**

## 9. Swordfighter Form (a Zelda transformation, not a separate unit)
- Stats: normally STR 6, MAG 34, SKL 19, SPD 16, DEF 8, RES 28, MOV 6. As a Swordfighter: STR 24, MAG 12, SKL 25, SPD 23, DEF 18, RES 16, MOV 7.
- **Energy:** starts at a maximum of 40; Might Crystals raise it.
  - It costs 5 Energy to activate, 10 per active turn, and Mighty weapon attacks cost extra.
  - It is restored by Still World Energy Orbs and some smoothies.
  - Zelda reverts automatically at 0 Energy.
- **While transformed:** Echo summoning and Bind are disabled, but Echoes already summoned stay.
- **Gear:** Swordfighter equipment upgrades are separate from ordinary weapon proficiency.

## 10. Bind and Reverse Bond (Tri Rod)
- Bind: moves an object or creature along with the caster.
- Reverse Bond: the caster moves along with a bound target.
- Shield Pull: removes compatible enemy shields.
- Weak Point Extraction: pulls detachable weak points off bosses.
- Object Suspension: holds light objects in the air.
- Enemy Displacement: pulls smaller enemies out of defensive formations.
- The target's size, CON and RES decide how hard it is to move.

## 13. Battlefield spaces
- Rift Boundary: blocks ordinary movement between the normal and Still World.
- Unstable Rift Space: damages ordinary units; can spread over time.
- Floating Island: isolated ground inside the Still World.
- Rotated Terrain: changes movement direction and climbing needs.
- Tri's Friend: a rescue objective needed to repair a Rift.
- Echo Learning Point: unlocks an object Echo.
- Bind Handle: lets attached objects be manipulated.
- Vertical Water Column: swim between elevations.
- Stamp Station: awards a stamp.
- Glowing Energy Orb: restores Swordfighter Energy.
- Rift Corruption Source: keeps making hostile Echoes until destroyed.
- Existing water, sand, ice, lava, pressure switches, climbing surfaces and Micro passages are reused.

## 14. Still World campaign
- Rifts consume parts of the overworld and replace ordinary encounters with Still World battles on broken islands, rewarding summoning, flying, climbing and cooperative movement.
- Unrepaired Rifts can spread after encounters, destroying shops, blocking recruitment and changing routes.
- Rift enemies get more RES and are immune to fear. Echoes cross unstable terrain unharmed.
- Rescuing Tri's friends restores regions; repairing Rifts gives Tri EXP, Might Crystals and regional rewards.

Regions:
1. Suthorn Forest and Ruins: Seismic Talus.
2. Gerudo Desert and Sanctum: Mogryph.

- Dampe's Studio: build and repair Automatons.
