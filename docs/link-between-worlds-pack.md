# A Link Between Worlds — Content Pack (user spec, condensed)

The pack covers Lorule, Ravio's rental shop, wall merging, painting magic and the relationship between the two kingdoms.
- Keep the roster small and reuse existing monsters and bosses. A different look or colour scheme is not enough for a new unit.
- Every unit is recruitable (the standing rule).
- The prices and the custom classes are proposed balancing values.

## 1. Returning units (no new entries)
- Yuga's ordinary monster forces come from the existing packs.
- From Era of Legend: Ganon, Helmasaur King, Arrghus, Blind the Thief, Trinexx, Buzz Blob, Anti-Fairy.
- From Original Zelda: Moldorm, Goriya, Rope, Zol and Gel.
- Stalfos and Moblin (Ocarina of Time); Darknut (Wind Waker); Hinox and Lynel (Wild era).
- Base roster: Octorok, Keese, ChuChus, Wizzrobe, Like Like, Gibdo, Wallmaster, Tektite.
- Also reused: Armos, Eyegore, the Minish Cap Ball and Chain Soldier, and Shadow Link (Four Swords Adventures).
- Mini-Moldorms use Moldorm with a reduced-size encounter modifier.
- Repeated boss appearances don't create new entries.
- **Art:** ganonALBW_alt.webp is an alt art for the Era of the Hero (Original Hyrule) Ganon, to be used in this campaign.
- Yuga Ganon gets his own unit: he combines two characters and has painting-based abilities.

- Biri (Micro, Common). Tiny Conductor: can share an enemy's space and shock it now and then.

## 4. Bosses
- **Margomill** (3, Legendary, Wind Guardian). Layered Body: rotating segments; each one destroyed lowers its DEF and raises its SPD.
  - Spinning Charge (6 STA), Wind Blast (10 MP), Rotating Defense (5 STA), Segment Regeneration (15 MP).
  - Weakness: wind attacks strip its segments.
- **Gemesaur King** (4, Legendary, Gemstone Beast). Gemstone Armor: its gem-covered mask absorbs damage until shattered, then its weak point is exposed. Separate from Helmasaur King because of the Rupee-producing gem armour.
  - Gemstone Barrage (10 MP), Crushing Charge (8 STA), Tail Sweep (6 STA), Gemstone Regrowth (15 MP).
- **Knucklemaster** (3, Legendary, Giant Hand). Crushing Palm: pins smaller units, which can't move until they escape or an ally intervenes.
  - Crushing Fist (8 STA), Grasp (6 STA), Sweeping Palm (7 STA), Wall Crush (10 STA).
  - Weakness: its exposed eye, and opponents that wall-merge.
- **Stalblind** (2, Legendary, Undead Guardian). Headless Warrior: detaches its head as a temporary summon that attacks on its own. Unlike Blind, it fights with sword and shield.
  - Shielded Slash (5 STA), Spectral Breath (10 MP), Detach Head (15 MP), Undead Reassembly (12 MP).
- **Zaganaga** (3, Legendary, Desert Plant). Rooted Ambush: relocates between connected sand spaces without crossing the terrain between.
  - Sand Burrow (5 STA), Spiked Seed Barrage (8 MP), Root Grasp (6 MP), Sandstorm (15 MP).
- **Dharkstare** (3, Legendary, Frozen Eye Demon). Frozen Shell: a regenerating ice barrier absorbs damage before its HP.
  - Ice Beam (8 MP), Frozen Prison (12 MP), Blizzard Field (15 MP), Ice Shell Regeneration (15 MP).
  - Weakness: fire destroys the shell.
- **Grinexx** (4, Legendary, Volcanic Turtle). Lava Dweller: submerges in lava and emerges beneath enemies; regenerates HP while submerged. Unlike Trinexx, it's about lava travel and eruptions.
  - Lava Geyser (12 MP), Shell Slam (8 STA), Submerge (5 STA), Volcanic Eruption (20 MP).
- **Yuga Ganon** (3, Mythic, Painting Demon King). Fusion of Power: Ganon's strength with Yuga's magic; can wall-merge despite his size.
  - Demonic Trident (8 STA), Lightning Barrage (12 MP), Painting Prison (15 MP), Dark World Merge (8 MP), Reflected Energy (10 MP), Triforce Cataclysm (30 MP).
  - Yuga and Yuga Ganon are both recruitable on their own, and a fusion event can turn Yuga into Yuga Ganon.
- **Minibosses:**
  - Heedle (2, Rare). Cyclone Swarm: summons small tornadoes that move on their own.
  - Gigabari (3, Epic). Electrical Division: splits into several Bari when destroyed.
  - Big Pengator (2, Rare). Ice Commander: strengthens nearby Pengators; heavy sliding charges.

## 7. Wall merging (a new movement state)
- It costs 5 MP to start, 2 MP per wall space travelled, and 5 MP to stay merged at the end of a turn.
- Merged units can't be targeted by ordinary melee. Wall-destroying attacks, specialised magic and painting attacks can still hit them.
- They must emerge on a valid empty space. Ordinary attacks and equipment are disabled while merged, unless compatible.
- Several units can merge on different faces of the same wall.
- At 0 MP the unit is forced out at the nearest valid space.
- Merged units occupy wall surfaces, not ground tiles.

### Painting Prison (new status)
- Lasts 3 turns. The unit can't move or attack, and is immune to ordinary physical attacks.
- It is freed by sacred magic, a painting dispel or the caster's defeat.
- Bosses get a shorter duration, or a temporary ability suppression instead.
- An imprisoned unit is alive and doesn't count as a casualty.

## 8. Ravio's rental shop
- Any item can be rented for the run or bought outright. Example: the Fire Rod rents for ₹100 or sells for ₹1,200.
- If the renter falls in battle, Sheerow reclaims the item. Purchases follow the normal recovery rules.
- Rentals use the existing item database, not separate rental copies.
- At camp, Ravio can move a rental between living units for free.

## 9. Maiamai rescue
- Baby Maiamai hide in ordinary battles, Micro passages, hidden caves and wall-merge areas.
- Every 10 rescued lets Mother Maiamai give one owned (bought) item a permanent Nice upgrade for the run. That's at 10, 20 … 90; at 100 the reward is the Great Spin upgrade.
- Rescued Maiamai don't join the army; recruitable Baby Maiamai are separate encounters with the same art.

## 11. Hyrule and Lorule campaign
- Two parallel kingdoms, each with its own people and politics (not the Light and Dark World mechanic). Travelling between them bypasses otherwise blocked encounters.
- Hyrule: more recruitment, trade and defence missions.
- Lorule: more dangerous encounters and rare equipment.

Regions:
- Hyrule:
  - Kakariko Village: merchants and recruitment.
  - Eastern Palace: Yuga.
  - House of Gales: Margomill.
  - Tower of Hera: Moldorm.
  - Sanctuary: Seres and sacred encounters.
  - Hyrule Castle: Yuga.
- Lorule:
  - Thieves' Town: Thief Girl.
  - Thieves' Hideout: Stalblind.
  - Dark Palace: Gemesaur King.
  - Swamp Palace: Arrghus.
  - Skull Woods: Knucklemaster.
  - Desert Palace: Zaganaga. Its route needs travel through both kingdoms; the entrance is in Hyrule.
  - Ice Ruins: Dharkstare.
  - Turtle Rock: Grinexx.
  - Treacherous Tower: a survival gauntlet.
  - Lorule Castle: Yuga Ganon.

New encounter types:
- Painting Rescue: free imprisoned allies or Sages.
- Parallel Infiltration: use both kingdoms to get past defences.
- Maiamai Rescue: find hidden Maiamai before the battle ends.
- Treacherous Tower: consecutive waves with limited recovery.
- Rental Challenge: win with assigned rental gear.
- Shadow Duel: fight a shadow opponent with a random loadout.

Camp facilities:
- Ravio's Shop: rent and buy.
- Maiamai Nursery: rescue rewards and Nice upgrades.
- Lorulean Forge: Master Ore enhancement.
- Milk Bar: consumables and support building.
- Sage Sanctuary: removes painting curses; better sacred magic.
