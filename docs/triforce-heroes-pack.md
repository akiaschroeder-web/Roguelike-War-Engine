# Tri Force Heroes — Content Pack (user spec, condensed)

A smaller expansion: cooperative three-unit formations (Totems), wearable outfits, material crafting and the kingdom of Hytopia.
- The ALBW Link is the Green Hero: his existing unit, unchanged stats.
- No new Zelda, Ganon or generic race. Existing monsters, bosses and equipment are reused.
- Every unit is recruitable (the standing rule). All stats and effects are custom adaptations.

## 6. Totem formation system (vertical stacking)
- Any compatible size-1 humanoids can take part, not only the heroes.
- Rules:
  - A Totem occupies one tile, up to 3 units tall.
  - The bottom unit controls movement, and the stack uses its lowest MOV.
  - The top unit can attack elevated targets. Middle units can use compatible ranged and support abilities.
  - Each member keeps their own HP, gear and actions.
  - The stack collapses if the bottom unit is defeated.
  - Micro units can ride Totem members without adding height.
- Actions:

| Action | Needs | Effect |
|---|---|---|
| Totem Stack | 2–3 units | Form a stack |
| Hero Throw | 2 | Throw the top unit across a gap or onto high ground |
| Elevated Strike | Height 2–3 | Hit an otherwise unreachable weak point |
| Triple Spin | 3 | Spin attacks at every level at once |
| Tri Force Assault | The three heroes | Combined attack with extra damage |
| Totem Guard | 2–3 | Protects the stacked units |

## 7. Battlefield spaces
- Elevated Switch: needs a Totem or a flier.
- Totem Platform: extra height for stacked units.
- Triforce Gateway: needs three units at once.
- Gripshot Target: crossing or cooperative pulling with the Gripshot.
- Water Pillar: temporary platform from the Water Rod.
- Outfit Pedestal: a temporary outfit for that encounter.

Existing switches, ice, lava, wind currents, moving platforms and formation spaces carry over.
