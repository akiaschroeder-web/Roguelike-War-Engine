# The Twilight Princess content pack, plus additional OoT/WW/TP units (design doc)

As supplied. Art in `art_sources/twilight_princess/`.

The Legend of Zelda: Twilight Princess — Content Pack

The Twilight Princess pack focuses on mounted combat, advanced sword techniques, transformation, shadow magic, heavy infantry and the interaction between the Light World and Twilight Realm.

Unlike the previous packs, there are no new generic racial unit sets. Existing Hylian, Goron, Zora and monster units cover the conventional army roles.

Link, Zelda and Ganondorf receive new incarnations. Other returning characters, monsters and equipment are not duplicated unless they represent distinctly different creatures or mechanics.

Every new unit, including bosses and alternate forms, is recruitable.

1. Main Characters

Link — Hero of Twilight

Race: Hylian Class: Twilight Swordsman Size: 1 Role: Versatile Infantry / Mounted Warrior

Personal Skill — Hidden Potential: Hidden Skills consume 25% less stamina. Successfully performing a Hidden Skill grants +2 SKL until the end of the following turn.

Proficiencies: Swords, greatswords, shields, bows, ball and chain weapons and mounted combat.

Wolf Link

Race: Transformed Hylian Class: Sacred Beast Size: 1 Role: Scout / Mobile Attacker

Personal Skill — Heightened Senses: Detects invisible enemies, buried items, spirits, hidden passages and traps within four spaces.

Natural abilities:

* Bite: Standard melee attack.
* Jump Attack: Leaps up to three spaces toward an enemy before attacking.
* Dig: Uncovers buried items and underground passages.
* Sense: Reveals concealed enemies and spirits.
* Howl: Activates Howling Stones and certain recruitment encounters.
* Energy Field: With Midna's assistance, marks and consecutively attacks up to five nearby enemies.

Wolf Link shares progression with Hero of Twilight when transformed but can also be obtained as an independently deployable unit.

Princess Zelda — Twilight Princess

Race: Hylian Class: Royal Light Mage Size: 1 Role: Magical Support / Ranged Attacker

Personal Skill — Light of Hyrule: Light attacks remove one beneficial magical effect from their target. Adjacent allies gain +3 RES against shadow magic.

Proficiencies: Rapiers, bows, staffs and sacred magic.

Ganondorf — Dark Lord

Race: Gerudo Class: Executioner King Size: 1 Role: Heavy Offensive Infantry

Personal Skill — Divine Defiance: The first otherwise fatal attack received each battle leaves Ganondorf with 1 HP and grants +5 STR and MAG for two turns.

Proficiencies: Greatswords, dark magic and mounted combat.

Dark Beast Ganon

Race: Demon Beast Class: Colossal Charger Size: 4 Role: Heavy Assault

Personal Skill — Beast of Darkness: Can charge through smaller enemy units, dealing damage and knocking them aside.

Natural abilities: Beast Charge, Savage Gore, Ground Slam and Shadow Portal.

This incarnation is a distinct quadrupedal form rather than the upright Ganon from Ocarina of Time.

19. Unique Combat Mechanics

Twilight corruption

Twilight corruption can spread across battlefield spaces through enemy abilities, environmental hazards and certain boss attacks.

Unit type	Effect inside Twilight corruption
Ordinary Light World units	−2 STR, −2 SPD and reduced visibility.
Twili and Twilight creatures	+2 MAG, +2 RES.
Wolf Link	Unaffected; can detect concealed spirits.
Light Spirits	Automatically purify occupied spaces.
Units carrying a Sol	Purify nearby spaces.

Twilight corruption is a battlefield hazard rather than a universal campaign restriction.

Mounted combat

Twilight Princess introduces additional techniques for the existing mount system.

Technique	Effect
Mounted Charge	Increases melee damage according to distance traveled before attacking.
Mounted Spin Attack	Strikes enemies on both sides of the mount.
Mounted Archery	Allows ranged attacks during movement with an accuracy penalty.
Mounted Grapple	Pulls an enemy from its mount.
Joust	Two mounted units clash, with damage and knockback determined by STR, CON and movement distance.
Forced Dismount	Separates a rider from their mount after a successful heavy attack.

These mechanics apply to existing mounted units from every content pack.

Shadow Beast revival

When a Shadow Beast is defeated, it enters a dormant state for one turn.

A surviving Shadow Beast can use its revival scream to restore dormant Shadow Beasts with 25% HP.

Defeating all remaining active Shadow Beasts before they can revive their allies ends the effect. Sacred finishing attacks also prevent revival.
