# The Minish Cap — Content Pack (user spec, condensed)

The Era of the Minish adds the Four Sword, Kinstone fusion and a new unit size: Micro units.
- Returning enemies and equipment reuse their existing entries.
- Link here is the Hero of the Minish.
- Every unit is recruitable, bosses and minibosses included.
- The Micro system is a permanent base-game addition, not limited to this campaign.
- The doc's own words: the four-per-tile limit, full deployment costs, the reduced damage against larger opponents, and the custom generic classes and stats are proposed gameplay adaptations.

## 1. Micro units (size 0)
### Occupancy
- Micro units don't block ordinary movement or deployment.
- Up to 4 Micro units can share one tile, regardless of allegiance.
- They can move through spaces held by allies and enemies.
- Each tile of a larger unit's footprint holds Micro units independently.
- They keep their own turns, equipment, stats and HP.
- They count toward army and deployment capacity.
- Opposing Micro units on the same tile can fight each other.

### Combat
| Interaction | Rule |
|---|---|
| Micro vs Micro | Standard |
| Micro vs Normal | Direct attacks deal 25% unless targeting a weak point |
| Normal vs Micro | Ordinary targeted attacks −60 Hit |
| Area attacks | Hit both normal and Micro units on the affected spaces |
| Weak-point attacks | Micro units bypass the size penalty by infiltrating vulnerable areas |
| Stomping | Larger units can deliberately try to crush Micro units sharing their spaces |
| Internal attacks | Some Micro units can enter compatible monsters or constructs and attack from within |

### Hitch Ride
- A Micro unit sharing an ally's space can ride along: it moves with the host for free.
- On its own turn it can dismount, attack or use support abilities.
- Examples: a Minish Blacksmith on a Goron, a Minish Archer on a flying Rito, Micro Link on a horse.

### Infiltration actions (on an enemy's tile)
Not every Micro unit learns every action.
- Sabotage: reduces equipment durability.
- Distract: lowers the enemy's SKL and Hit.
- Trip: lowers the enemy's MOV, or interrupts its movement.
- Expose Weak Point: lowers the enemy's DEF for the attacks that follow.
- Internal Strike: hits a compatible enemy's internal weak point.
- Pickpocket: steals an eligible unequipped pack item.

## 5. Bosses (all recruitable)
- **Big Green ChuChu** (size 3, Legendary, Giant Slime).
  - Gelatinous Giant: −40% physical damage unless knocked over; double damage to Micro units.
  - Abilities: Crushing Bounce, Gelatinous Slam, Acid Splash, Body Regeneration.
  - Weakness: Gust Jar destabilises it and exposes its head.
- **Big Blue ChuChu** (size 3, Legendary, Giant Electric Slime; miniboss).
  - Gigantic Charge: electrifies every space in its footprint, hurting enemies and Micro units there.
  - Abilities: Thunder Bounce, Electrical Discharge, Static Field, Gel Regeneration.
  - Weakness: Gust Jar while its coating is inactive.
- **Big Octorok** (size 3, Legendary, Frost Beast).
  - Frozen Arena: freezes surrounding terrain; +8 DEF/RES while frozen.
  - Abilities: Boulder Spit, Freezing Breath, Ice Charge, Vacuum Inhale, Ink Cloud.
  - Weakness: fire on the flower on its back disables the frozen armour.
- **Gleerok** (size 4, Legendary, Volcanic Beast).
  - Volcanic Shell: heavy armour until its shell mechanism is struck by a compatible tool.
  - Abilities: Lava Breath, Shell Defense, Crushing Neck, Volcanic Eruption.
- **Mazaal** (size 4, Legendary, Ancient Construct).
  - Mechanical Interior: its outer body resists most damage; Micro units can enter its head and destroy the internal mechanisms.
  - Abilities: Crushing Hands, Eye Beam, Mechanical Slam, Internal Defense.
- **Gyorg pair.**
  - Female Gyorg (size 4, Legendary): a large flying platform with several vulnerable eyes. Micro units and ordinary infantry can ride her back, making her a mobile aerial platform.
  - Male Gyorg (size 3, Legendary): a fast aerial attacker that protects the female.
  - Shared skill, Aerial Pair: +3 STR/SPD/DEF when both are deployed.
- **Vaati Reborn** (size 2, Mythic, Demonic Sorcerer).

  - Abilities: Dark Orb, Eye Barrier, Shadow Flame, Magical Teleport.

- **Vaati's Wrath** (size 4, Mythic, Wind Demon).

- **Minibosses:**
  - Madderpillar (Micro, Rare, Insect Bruiser).

  - Silver Darknut (2, Epic, Armored Swordsman).

  - Red Darknut (2, Epic, Assault Knight).

## 11. Mechanics
### Shrinking and growing
- Portals and some abilities shrink normal units. The unit keeps its level, EXP, supports, abilities and HP%.
- Modifiers for units without their own Micro stats: HP ×0.8; STR unchanged against Micro targets; MAG and RES unchanged; SKL +2; SPD +3; DEF ×0.6; CON 1–2; MOV +1.
- Micro Link uses his own stat profile.
- Growing needs enough free space for the returning unit; a unit can't grow inside a creature or a solid obstacle.

### Micro dungeons
- Some encounters are Micro-only: pests in a house, a library bookshelf, a flooded garden, infiltrating a mechanical guardian, fighting a normal-sized monster from a Micro perspective.
- Normal-sized enemies keep their usual entries, handled by the Micro combat rules.

### Four Sword duplication
- The infusion stage sets the number of duplicates: 1 at two elements, 2 at three, 3 at four.
- Cost 20 MP; duplicates last 3 turns.
- Duplicates have 40% of the wielder's max HP and 60% of their offensive stats.
- They can move, attack, activate switches and surround enemies.
- They can't use consumables, trade equipment or make more duplicates.
- They take no deployment slot, give no EXP when destroyed and are never permanent casualties.

### Kinstone fusion and figurines
- Fusions happen at camp, at recruitment encounters or with battlefield NPCs.
- Possible outcomes:
  - treasure chests in regions already visited;
  - hidden caves opening;
  - rare monster spawns;
  - new recruits;
  - Great Fairy encounters.
- Gold Kinstones tie to specific major quests instead of random rewards.
- Figurine Gallery: Carlov trades figurines for Mysterious Shells. They unlock artwork, character descriptions, monster information and completion milestones, with no combat effect.
