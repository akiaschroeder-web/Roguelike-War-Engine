# The Legend of Zelda — Era of the Wild Content Pack (Breath of the Wild + Tears of the Kingdom)

Design doc as supplied by the user (condensed into compact form; every value kept). Build after the Skyward Sword pack, once all art has arrived.

Focus: open warfare, Sheikah technology, Zonai construction, elemental combat, weapon fusion, environmental interaction, vertical battlefields. Characters in both games share one entry unless substantially different. Reuse existing monsters/equipment/materials. All bosses, Divine Beasts, Constructs and combat units are recruitable. Rito/Zora/Goron/Gerudo/Korok already have generic units; the Yiga Clan and Zonai Constructs add new military unit types.

## 1. Main Characters

**Link — Hero of the Wild.** Hylian, Champion of Hyrule, size 1, Legendary, Adaptive Infantry. Personal — Resourceful Warrior: switch equipped weapons without a primary action; fused weapons +20% durability. Abilities: Flurry Rush (multiple counters after a successful dodge); Perfect Guard (parry, maybe reflect projectiles); Bullet Time (slow enemy reactions during aerial ranged attacks); Charged Attack (weapon-specific heavy attack); Champion's Arsenal (lower stamina costs using multiple weapon categories). Uses both Sheikah Slate and Zonai Arm abilities via equipped tools.

**Zelda — Princess of the Wild.** Hylian, Sacred Scholar, size 1, Legendary, Sacred Magic / Research Support. Personal — Awakening Power: sacred abilities grow stronger as allies take damage during the battle. Abilities: Sealing Light (massive sacred damage vs corrupted); Sacred Protection (barrier on an ally); Ancient Research (reveals mechanical enemies' weaknesses); Light of Restoration (removes Gloom and Malice); Recall Blessing (temporarily restores an ally's previous position).

**Ganondorf — Demon King.** Gerudo / Demon, Gloom Sovereign, size 2, Mythic, Heavy Duelist / Corruption. Personal — Demon King's Dominion: attacks inflict Gloom (temporary max HP reduction); nearby demonic units +4 STR/MAG. Abilities: Gloom Blade (reduces max HP); Demon King's Spear (long reach pierce); Gloom Barrage (multiple projectiles); Perfect Evasion (dodge and counter a melee attack); Phantom Legion (summons temporary Phantom Ganondorfs). Can transform into the Demon Dragon via the Secret Stone ability.

**Calamity Ganon.** Malice Entity, Calamity Incarnate, size 4, Mythic, Siege Monster / Corruption. Personal — Calamitous Arsenal: alternates ancient mechanical weapons and elemental attacks without an equipment-switch action. Abilities: Ancient Laser, Malice Eruption, Calamity Cleaver, Elemental Barrage, Guardian Corruption.

**Dark Beast Ganon — Calamity Form.** Malice Entity, Calamity Colossus, size 5, Mythic, Heavy Siege. Personal — Hatred Unleashed: ordinary attacks on its body −75% damage; exposed Malice weak points take extra sacred damage. Abilities: Calamity Beam, Malice Stampede, Corrupting Presence, Dark Eruption. Distinct from TP's Dark Beast Ganon: a huge ranged siege creature, not a mobile duelist.

## 2. Champions and Sages

Champions (all Legendary): Mipha (Zora, Champion Healer, 1), Daruk (Goron, Champion Defender, 2), Revali (Rito, Champion Archer, 1), Urbosa (Gerudo, Thunder Champion, 1).
- Mipha — Mipha's Grace: once per battle prevents a fatal attack on herself or an ally within 3, restoring 40% max HP. Abilities: Healing Waters, Lightscale Thrust, Aquatic Dash, Restorative Aura.
- Daruk — Daruk's Protection: starts each battle with 3 charges, each fully blocks one damaging attack. Abilities: Boulder Breaker, Goron Charge, Protective Dome, Seismic Slam.
- Revali — Revali's Gale: updraft 3×/battle for himself or a compatible unit to gain elevation and perform aerial attacks. Abilities: Aerial Volley, Triple Shot, Diving Arrow, Wind Barrage.
- Urbosa — Urbosa's Fury: charged melee attacks can release lightning on all enemies within 3; 3 charges/battle. Abilities: Thunder Slash, Gerudo Counter, Lightning Field, Scimitar Dance.

Modern Sages (all Legendary): Sidon (Zora, Sage of Water, 2), Yunobo (Goron, Sage of Fire, 2), Tulin (Rito, Sage of Wind, 1), Riju (Gerudo, Sage of Lightning, 1), Mineru (Zonai, Sage of Spirit, 1).
- Sidon — Water Sage's Vow: water barrier on himself or an adjacent ally; blocks one attack or discharges as a ranged water slash. Abilities: Royal Trident, Water Shield, Tidal Slash, Aquatic Rescue.
- Yunobo — Fire Sage's Vow: rolling attacks destroy cracked walls, ore deposits and breakables, dealing fire damage. Abilities: Flaming Roll, Rock Breaker, Volcanic Charge, Goron Guard.
- Tulin — Wind Sage's Vow: directional gust extends allied aerial movement by 4. Abilities: Precision Shot, Wind Gust, Aerial Pursuit, Eagle Eye.
- Riju — Lightning Sage's Vow: expanding lightning field; an allied projectile hit on an enemy inside triggers a lightning strike. Abilities: Twin Scimitar Strike, Lightning Field, Thunderbolt, Gerudo Rally.
- Mineru — Spirit Sage's Vow: possess compatible mechanical bodies; operate Zonai equipment with less energy. Abilities: Spirit Transfer, Construct Control, Ancient Knowledge, Zonai Enhancement.

**Mineru's Construct.** Zonai Construct, Modular War Machine, size 2, Mythic. Modular Arsenal: attach weapons or Zonai Devices to each arm and its back. Slots: Left Arm, Right Arm, Back Device, Power Core. Mineru can transfer into it (shared progression and supports); also independently recruitable.

## 3. Ancient Hyrule

- **Rauru — First King of Hyrule.** Zonai, King of Light, size 2, Mythic, Sacred Magic / Heavy Support. Light of the Zonai: sacred attacks ignore 30% RES, extra damage vs Gloom-inflicted targets. Abilities: Sacred Beam, Light Barrier, Divine Arm, Royal Seal. Distinct from OoT's Rauru.
- **Sonia.** Hylian, Queen of Time, size 1, Legendary, Time Magic / Support. Temporal Wisdom: once per round, restore a nearby ally's previous position without using its movement. Abilities: Recall, Temporal Restoration, Time Barrier, Sacred Guidance.
- **The Light Dragon.** Immortal Dragon, Sacred Sky Guardian, size 5, Mythic. Eternal Restoration: regenerates 15% max HP per turn; nearby allies heal and gradually lose Gloom. Abilities: Sacred Breath, Healing Wind, Divine Flight, Restorative Scales. Shares Zelda's identity and supports when reached by transformation.
- **The Demon Dragon.** Immortal Demon Dragon, Dragon of Gloom, size 5, Mythic. Eternal Calamity: leaves Gloom terrain on its flight path; ordinary healing can't restore max HP lost to its attacks. Abilities: Gloom Meteor, Demonic Breath, Corrupting Flight, Calamity Dive. Shares Ganondorf's progression when transformed.
- Draconification is irreversible in the source; here reversal needs a special sacred event, not a free transformation.

## 25. Combat Mechanics
Gloom vs Malice: both deal HP damage, corrupt machinery and spread across terrain. Only Gloom reduces max HP; ordinary healing works on Malice but only restores undamaged max HP for Gloom. Counters: Malice → sacred attacks; Gloom → sacred light, sunlight, Sundelions. Gloom-lost max HP returns via sunlight, Lightroots, Light Dragon abilities or meals.
Elemental reactions: Fire+Grass spreading fire · Fire+Pine Cone strong updraft · Fire+Ice melts to water · Ice+Water frozen surface · Lightning+Water electrifies connected water · Lightning+Metal conducts through connected metal · Water+Sludge removes sludge · Wind+Fire spreads flames directionally · Wind+Smoke disperses/redirects concealment · Heat+Balloon lift · Ice+Lava temporary solid terrain.
Flurry Rush (after a successful perfect dodge): one-handed sword 4 rapid strikes · two-handed 2 powerful · spear 5 thrusts · natural unit-specific counter. Individual strikes deal reduced damage so it stays strong but not multiplied excessively.
Champion abilities are limited-use; Sage abilities repeat with cooldowns: Mipha's Grace 1/battle · Daruk's Protection 3 charges · Revali's Gale 3 · Urbosa's Fury 3 · Sidon's Water Shield 3-turn cooldown · Yunobo's Charge 2 · Tulin's Gust 2 · Riju's Lightning 3 · Mineru's Construct persistent while deployed.
Blood Moon: periodic on the campaign map: renewed monster encounters, replenished monster resources, stronger elite recruitment, critical cooking; defeated regional bosses may return as optional fights. Fallen allies are not resurrected (permadeath stays).
