# The Legend of Zelda: Skyward Sword — Content Pack

## User adjustments (given with the doc)

- **Scrap the Great Spirit of the Skies** (it would just be Levias twice).
- **Retire the old Loftwing Mount**; reuse its art for the **Crimson Loftwing**.

The pack focuses on aerial combat, weapon crafting, stamina management, ancient technology, time manipulation and divine weaponry. Smaller than Twilight Princess: important characters, distinctive monsters and bosses, no new generic racial unit sets. Returning monsters and equipment use existing entries unless functionally different. All units (bosses and divine creatures included) are independently recruitable.

## 1. Main Characters

**Link — Hero of the Sky.** Hylian, Knight of the Goddess, size 1, Legendary, Versatile Swordsman.
Personal — Chosen by the Goddess: sword proficiency XP +50%; fully charged sword techniques +4 Might.
Abilities: Skyward Strike (charge, ranged slash); Fatal Blow (finish a knocked-down enemy); Shield Bash (parry, stagger); Spin Attack (surrounding); Dash (stamina for extra movement).
Proficiencies: swords, shields, bows, adventuring tools, Loftwing riding.

**Zelda — Incarnation of Hylia.** Hylian / Divine Incarnation, Sacred Priestess, size 1, Mythic, Divine Support.
Personal — Goddess Reborn: sacred abilities cost 25% less MP; nearby allies +4 RES vs demonic enemies.
Abilities: Goddess's Blessing (heal + remove curses); Sacred Seal (target can't move or transform, temporary); Divine Barrier (nearby allies protected from magic); Prayer of Hylia (restore sacred energy and MP to allies).

**Fi.** Sword Spirit, Divine Guide, size 1, Legendary, Magical Support / Analysis.
Personal — Probability Analysis: reveals enemy stats, weaknesses, equipment, abilities; allies +15 Hit vs analyzed targets.
Abilities: Dowsing (locate hidden enemies, treasure, objectives); Divine Dance (nearby allies +SKL, +Avoid); Sacred Blade (ranged light damage); Sword Communion (ally's sword +Might, +Hit, temporary).
Can deploy independently or be assigned to a compatible divine sword as a supporting spirit.

**Groose.** Hylian, Siege Engineer, size 1, Epic, Heavy Support / Artillery.
Personal — Groosenator: siege weapons +5 Might, +20 Hit when he operates them.
Abilities: Bombardment; Construct Emplacement (stationary artillery platform); Heavy Lift (move large objects); Unexpected Courage (+4 STR/DEF when defending an injured ally).

**Impa — Guardian of Hylia.** Sheikah, Sacred Guardian, size 1, Legendary, Protective Infantry / Magic. Original entry, separate from OoT Impa.
Personal — Sacred Duty: once per round, intercepts an attack on an adjacent allied priestess or divine unit.
Abilities: Sacred Barrier, Sheikah Strike, Shadow Dash, Protective Seal.

**Ghirahim.** Demon Sword Spirit, Demon Lord, size 1, Legendary, Duelist / Magical Assassin.
Personal — Predictive Guard: first ordinary melee attack on him each round has 50% to be caught and negated.
Abilities: Blade Catch (parry, maybe disarm); Demon Darts (spread of projectiles); Teleport Slash (teleport adjacent then attack); Demonic Restoration (recover HP from dark energy).
Can transform into his true form.

**Ghirahim — True Form.** Demon Sword Spirit, Living Demon Blade, size 1, Mythic, Heavy Duelist.
Personal — Metallic Body: ordinary physical damage −40%; sacred attacks bypass.
Abilities: Demon Blade, Rapid Teleportation, Diamond Barrage, Executioner's Strike. Shares progression with normal Ghirahim.

**Demise.** Demon Deity, Demon King, size 2, Mythic, Heavy Infantry / Dark Magic. Separate from Ganondorf.
Personal — Eternal Hatred: each 25% of max HP lost → +2 STR and MAG for the battle.
Abilities: Demon King's Slash; Lightning Sword (charge blade with lightning); Dark Skyward Strike (ranged slash); Demonic Dominion (nearby demonic allies +3 STR/RES).

## 20. Unique Mechanics

**Timeshift fields.** Present → ancient: dormant robots → reactivated; dried waterways → flowing water; broken machinery → working; collapsed barriers → restored; Technoblin remains → active Technoblins; dried vegetation → living. Recruited ancient units work outside fields but gain +3 DEF/RES inside one.

**Directional combat.** Selective: Quadro Babas, Ghirahim, Stalmasters and similar defend specific directions (up, down, horizontal, vertical, diagonal, thrust). Attacking an unguarded direction bypasses the stance. Not applied to every ordinary attack.

**Silent Realm encounters.** Collect the required Sacred Tears; avoid Watchers and dormant Guardians; a tear temporarily pacifies Guardians; Waking Water or detection activates them; the trial participant can't fight; success unlocks sacred equipment, medals or permanent enhancements. Guardians met in ordinary combat stay recruitable.

**Skyward Strike.** Charge 1 action: +3 Mt, range 2–3 · 2 actions: +6 Mt, range 2–5 · 3 actions: +10 Mt, range 2–6. Upgraded Goddess Swords and the enhanced Master Sword charge faster. Activates Goddess Cubes and sacred mechanisms.
