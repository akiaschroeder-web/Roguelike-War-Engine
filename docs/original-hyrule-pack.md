# The Legend of Zelda — Original Hyrule Pack (1986)

Design doc as supplied by the user (condensed; every value kept). The user asked for it to be built alongside Era of the Wild.

Small pack: classic dungeon crawling, simple equipment, monsters not in earlier packs. Returning monsters use existing units even where the NES look differs. Link, Zelda and Ganon are exceptions to the returning-character rule (new entries).

## 1. Main Characters
**Link — Hero of Hyrule.** Hylian, Adventurer, size 1, Legendary, Versatile Infantry. Personal — It's Dangerous to Go Alone: +2 STR/DEF/SKL when no allies within 3. Abilities: Sword Beam (ranged projectile at full HP); Dungeon Explorer (reveals hidden doors/passages within 3); Quick Item (once per battle, use a pack item without a primary action); Triforce's Courage (temporary immunity to fear and confusion).

**Princess Zelda — Era of Decline.** Hylian, Princess of Wisdom, size 1, Legendary, Magical Support. Personal — Scattered Wisdom: each Triforce of Wisdom fragment collected gives her a cumulative +1 MAG and RES for the rest of the run. Abilities: Light of Wisdom (reveals invisible enemies and hidden objects); Sacred Barrier (protects an ally from magic); Wisdom's Guidance (allies +SKL, +LCK); Triforce Restoration (nearby allies recover HP and MP).

**Ganon — Prince of Darkness.** Demon, Invisible Sorcerer, size 2, Mythic, Magical Ambusher. Personal — Invisible Darkness: invisible between attacks; sacred light and detection temporarily reveal him. Abilities: Dark Fireball; Invisible Assault (attack from concealment, more crit); Dark Teleport (up to 5); Prince of Darkness (nearby monsters +3 STR/MAG). Silver Arrows deal extra damage to him and prevent his invisibility for 3 turns.

## 2. Supporting Characters (all recruitable)
Old Man (Hermit, 1, Rare — Ancient Advice: reveals hidden dungeon objectives and enemy weaknesses; can use magical staffs) · Old Woman (Potion Maker, 1, Rare — Life Medicine: potions +30%; healing and consumables) · Merchant (Traveling Trader, 1, Uncommon — Secret Merchandise: discounted equipment, sometimes reveals rare items; economic support).

## 3. New Monsters
- Goriya (1, Uncommon, Boomerang Fighter) — Returning Strike: boomerangs hit on the way out and back. Abilities: Boomerang Throw, Returning Slash, Disarming Throw.
- Rope (1, Common, Serpent) — Sudden Charge: +4 MOV when an enemy is directly in front. Abilities: Venomous Bite, Serpent Charge, Coil.
- Zol (1, Common, Dividing Slime) — Division: on fatal damage splits into two temporary Gels unless the hit was strong enough. Abilities: Slime Slam, Body Division, Engulf.
- Gel (1, Common, Lesser Slime) — Clinging Body: contact attacks −1 enemy MOV for a turn. Abilities: Sticky Collision, Cling, Slime Merge.
- Vire (1, Rare, Winged Demon) — Bat Division: splits into two temporary Keese when killed by ordinary attacks. Abilities: Demonic Leap, Claw Strike, Bat Division.
- Pols Voice (1, Uncommon, Leaping Beast) — Sensitive Hearing: double damage from offensive musical abilities and arrows; resists ordinary melee. Abilities: Leaping Collision, Piercing Squeal, Erratic Hop.
- Ghini (1, Uncommon, Graveyard Spirit) — Graveyard Haunting: summons a temporary Ghini from an adjacent gravestone. Abilities: Spectral Collision, Grave Summoning, Phantom Passage.
- Lanmola (2, Rare, Segmented Crawler) — Segmented Body: segments absorb damage before the main body. Abilities: Burrowing Charge, Segmented Sweep, Rapid Crawl.
- Patra (3, Legendary, Swarm Controller) — Orbital Defense: eight small flying eyes intercept attacks; destroyed eyes don't regenerate that battle. Abilities: Expanding Orbit, Contracting Orbit, Eye Swarm, Central Eye Blast.
Blue/Red Goriyas and Lanmolas use the existing monster progression tiers.

## 4. New Bosses
- **Aquamentus** (3, Legendary, Unicorn Dragon, Magical Artillery) — Triple Fireball: ranged magic fires three spreading projectiles. Abilities: Triple Fireball (three lanes), Horn Charge (charge, knockback), Dragon Guard (temporary +DEF/RES), Concentrated Breath (three projectiles as one).
- **Manhandla** (3, Legendary, Four-Headed Plant, Multi-Directional Assault) — Frenzied Growth: +2 SPD, +1 MOV per destroyed head. Abilities: Four-Way Assault, Devouring Bite (heal from melee damage), Seed Barrage, Frenzied Charge. Each head has its own HP; destroying all four defeats it.
- **Digdogger** (4, Legendary, Giant Eye Monster, Heavy Assault) — Resonant Weakness: the Recorder shrinks it, exposing its small vulnerable form. Abilities: Rolling Collision, Heavy Impact (knock back adjacent), Eye Fixation (mark: more damage), Fragmentation (splits into three smaller Digdoggers).
- **Lesser Digdogger** (1, Rare, Fragmented Eye) — Erratic Movement: +20 Avoid after moving 3+ spaces. Independently recruitable and produced by Fragmentation.
Returning bosses use existing units: Dodongo, Gohma (Queen Gohma / existing Gohma), Gleeok (existing variants; the classic one = Flame Gleeok), Moldorm.

## 6. Equipment
Weapons (type, rarity, Mt, effect): White Sword (sword, R, 12, sword beams at full HP) · Magical Sword (sword, L, 22, powerful beams, more sacred damage) · Magical Rod (staff, E, 14, magic energy; works with the Book of Magic) · Magical Boomerang (boomerang, R, 8, more range and stun) · Goriya Boomerang (boomerang, U, 6, hits on return) · Aquamentus Horn (natural, L, 18, three projectiles) · Manhandla Fang (natural, E, 14, heals 15% of damage dealt) · Patra's Eye (magical, L, 17, stronger while orbital eyes survive). The starting Sword, Bow and Boomerang reuse existing entries.
Shields/armor: Magical Shield (off hand, E: +8 DEF, +6 RES, blocks compatible magic projectiles) · Green Clothes (body, C: light adventurer clothing) · Blue Ring (accessory, E: incoming damage −25%) · Red Ring (accessory, L: −50%). Rings don't stack.
Accessories/tools: Power Bracelet (existing) · Raft (U: travel designated water routes) · Stepladder (R: cross one-space gaps and narrow water) · Recorder (E: wind fast travel; exposes Digdogger) · Book of Magic (E: Magical Rod projectiles leave burning terrain) · Magical Key (L: opens ordinary locked doors without Small Keys) · Letter (U: unlocks the Old Woman's medicine services) · Blue Candle (U: fire once per battle) · Red Candle (R: fire repeatedly, 2-turn cooldown) · Triforce of Wisdom Fragment (L: cumulative magic bonus) · Triforce of Wisdom (Mythic: +8 MAG, +8 RES, +5 LCK, reveals concealed enemies; assembled from eight fragments; if an equivalent complete Triforce accessory exists, fragments are another way to get it).

## 8. Battlefield Spaces
Burnable Bush (burning may reveal treasure, stairs or a passage) · Movable Boulder (needs STR or Power Bracelet) · Hidden Staircase (secret chamber, merchant or treasure) · Bombable Wall (explosives reveal another room) · Raft Dock (reach islands and dungeon entrances) · Ladder Crossing (Stepladder bridges one space) · Push Block (activates mechanisms or reveals stairs) · Shutter Door (closed until the room objective is done) · Blade Trap (slides toward units entering its lane) · Hungry Goriya Checkpoint (needs Enemy Bait or combat) · Secret Cave (NPC, treasure or event). Overlaps with existing cracked walls, locked doors and switches reuse those systems with an NES look.

## 9. Dungeon Campaign
Level 1 Eagle → Aquamentus · 2 Moon → Dodongo · 3 Manji → Manhandla · 4 Snake → Gleeok · 5 Lizard → Digdogger · 6 Dragon → Gohma · 7 Demon → Aquamentus · 8 Lion → Gleeok · 9 Death Mountain → Ganon. Each of the first eight awards a Triforce of Wisdom fragment; the full Triforce unlocks Death Mountain.
Second Quest (optional difficulty modifier): rearranged layouts, changed boss placements, secret entrances moved, harder formations, more invisible walls and hidden passages, extra Hero Medals on completion.
